# Modular Air Filtration & Odor Extraction Projects

A collection of personal, open-source projects exploring 3D-printed air filtration and odor extraction systems. Built around readily available components, embedded control, and customizable modular designs.

---

# Projects

## 1. Smart Cat Litter Odor Extractor

A compact, 3D-printed odor extraction system designed to reduce unpleasant odors around a cat litter box. Uses activated carbon filtration and a microcontroller-controlled fan to automate extraction based on detected activity.

### Goals
- Compact and modular design
- Automated odor extraction
- Replaceable activated carbon filter
- Minimal proprietary components
- Easy to print, assemble, and maintain

### Features
- Fully 3D-printed housing
- Raspberry Pi Pico W controller
- Activated carbon pellet filtration
- PIR-based motion detection
- Automated fan control with configurable run-on time
- 12V DC power input with regulated low-voltage power for electronics

### Bill of Materials
- 12V DC power supply
- 12V to 3.3V buck converter
- 120 mm PC case fan
- PIR motion sensor
- Activated carbon pellets
- PLA and TPU filament

## 2. Modular Desktop Air Purifier

A modular desktop air purifier designed for makers and 3D-printing environments. Built around readily available PC components and fully 3D-printable parts for easy assembly, maintenance, and repair.

<img width="2708" height="1246" alt="Desktop air purifier" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/28ef18d0-f6f6-435c-80c9-e105b5e2b50a" />

### Goals
- Modular and repairable design
- Uses off-the-shelf components
- Minimal proprietary parts
- Easy to print and assemble
- Open-source and customizable

### Features
- Fully 3D-printable modular body
- Raspberry Pi Pico W controller
- Standard 120 mm PC case fan mounting (PWM and RGB compatible)
- Embedded magnet system for tool-free assembly and easy part replacement
- Easily replaceable HEPA filter
- Replaceable activated carbon pellet filter for VOC and odor reduction
- Washable fabric pre-filter
- Locally hosted MQTT server for fan speed and RGB control
- Photoresistor-based RGB auto shut-off

### Bill of Materials
- 12V DC barrel connector and AC adapter
- 12V to 5V buck converter
- 3x 120 mm standard PC case fans
- 16x 6x2 mm neodymium magnets
- TPU and PLA filament
